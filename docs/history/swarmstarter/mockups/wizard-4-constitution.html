Constitution and treasury

Write the rules the governor enforces and decide how money is held and released.

Community constitution
Art. 1
Purpose and scope
Build the RPG described in the ratified spec. Out of scope changes need a proposal.
Edit
Art. 2
Treasury use
Funds may pay for compute, model credits, bounties and audits. No salaries above the cap in Art. 6.
Edit
Art. 3
Proposals and voting
Any verified backer may propose. Quorum 10% of eligible voters, simple majority, 72 hour window.
Edit
Art. 4
Governor
The governor rules on every proposal and treasury action against this constitution.
Edit
Art. 5
Appeals
Any party may appeal a ruling within 72 hours to a Verafy judge panel. Panel ruling is final and sealed.
