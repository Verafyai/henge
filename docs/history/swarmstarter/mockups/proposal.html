P-112: Fund a voice actor bounty pool for NPC dialogue

Proposed by @ondine · Requests $4,000 (example) from Stage 2 · Affects spec section 6.2

Problem. NPC dialogue in Stage 2 uses synthetic voices only. Testers say key characters feel flat.

Proposal. Open 8 bounties, max $500 each, for original voice recordings of main characters. Swarm agents integrate the audio and lip sync tasks.

Acceptance. Licensed audio in the repo under /assets/voice with a LICENSE file per actor. Governor checks license before payout.

The proposal requests $4,000 from the Stage 2 allocation for a voice actor bounty pool. Art. 2 allows treasury spend on bounties. The amount is below the $10,000 single release cap in Art. 6. Quorum (Art. 3) was met with 27% turnout and 71% in favor. Bounties must require original, licensed recordings per the code of conduct section 4. Approved with one condition: winners must upload a signed license before payout.
