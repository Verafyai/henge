Governor and auto-mod

Bring your own keys. The governor enforces the constitution and records every decision.

Governor
Neutral third-party AI that rules on proposals, treasury actions and stage acceptance.
Required

Encrypted with a per-project key (KMS envelope). Never shown again after save. Only the governor worker can decrypt it.

✓ Key valid · model reachable · test ruling on a sample proposal returned in 6.2s
Auto-moderator
Screens forum posts, suggestions and Discord or Telegram messages against the code of conduct.
Optional

Auto-mod only hides or flags. Bans need a human moderator. Every action goes to the governor log.

Why a separate governor?

The creator should not grade their own work. The governor runs on a neutral provider with a model chosen by vote, and its prompts and inputs are public.

Misbehaving governor?

Any ruling can be appealed to Verafy judges. Two overturned rulings in 30 days triggers a vote to swap the model.

Who pays? The key owner pays the provider. The treasury can reimburse governor costs if Art. 2 allows it.
